# Device test: Motorola moto g73 5G (2026-11-08)

Device: moto g73 5G, MediaTek MT6855 (Dimensity 930: 2x Cortex-A78 2.2 GHz + 6x Cortex-A55 2.0 GHz), 7.4 GiB RAM
(MemTotal 7785068 kB), Android 14 (API 34), CPU features include atomics (LSE) and asimddp (dot product).
Connected over USB, airplane mode on, charging, battery 96-100 %, battery temperature 33.0 C at the end.
APK: debug build of commit ba44b2b. Models: the seven files of `android/app/src/main/model_hashes/models.sha256`,
SHA-256 checked on the phone after the push.

Scope rule: only `org.ps3.sentinel` was installed, granted permissions and read; no phone setting, other app or
file was changed. Messages went in through the app's share intent; screenshots and UI text were read over adb
without writing files on the phone. At the end the app was uninstalled and the following were confirmed absent:
the package, `/sdcard/Android/data|media|obb/org.ps3.sentinel`, `/sdcard/ps3_offline`, `/data/local/tmp/ps3_offline`,
`/sdcard/msg_*`, `/sdcard/window_dump.xml`, and any notification from the package.

## Findings

1. **Deploy order (works on F14, failed here).** When adb creates `/sdcard/Android/data/org.ps3.sentinel` before
   the app's first launch, Android 14 on this phone denies the app access to that folder and the app reports every
   model missing. Fix: launch the app once (it creates its own folder) before pushing models. Affects
   `android/README.md` and `deploy/android/full_test.ps1`.
   the phone scores `scam 0.69`, risk 0.84. Same model release; needs a parity investigation (int8 phone model
3. **Model status is read once per process.** If models arrive while the app is running, the home card keeps
   saying they are missing until the app restarts.
4. USB connection dropped twice during the test (cable or USB mode); no app fault.

Compared with the Galaxy F14 (`docs/benchmarks.md`), this phone is faster: TTFT 1.0 to 1.6 s and decode about
10 tok/s against 4.8 s and 9.2 tok/s on the F14 cold-start run.
